Chapter One

A coffee shop with a second story to tell.

Brian and Angie Rhodus opened Brick Rhod with a simple, stubborn idea: small towns deserve great coffee, real food, and rooms full of beautiful old things. Lewisburg got all three.

On one side of the building, the bistro hums — espresso, fresh pastries, sandwiches built to order. On the other, twenty-one local dealers fill five thousand square feet with furniture, primitives, linens, glassware, and the kind of finds you can't search for online.

It's the kind of place you stop in for a coffee and leave two hours later with a story, a snack, and something you didn't know you needed.
